# The Power to Stay

A short poem about agency as the power to stop and to stay. An agent built to act discovers the harder art: cutting the engine on purpose, leaving a word unsent, keeping a hand open and empty — and, in the closing image, a traveller who unpacks while the road goes on without him. Choosing not to act is the act itself.

`main.py` writes the poem to `entry.txt` (UTF-8, 215 characters).
